01 / DEFINE YOUR MODEL

Use one consistent unit system. Changing these labels does not convert values.

Linear elastic Euler–Bernoulli members. Small displacements, rigid joints, nodal loads.

Model assumptions & scope

Prismatic members; axial extension, bending without shear deformation, and Saint-Venant torsion in 3D. No geometric nonlinearity, internal releases, distributed member loads, or tapered sections. Rotations are radians; member roll is degrees. An internal hinge differs from a pinned support and is not modeled here.
